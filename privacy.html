Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

WhatsAppArchive Collector is a personal, single-user tool operated for its owner's own archive. This policy describes the tool's intended processing when the owner connects WhatsApp and Google Drive.

Data processed

The collector reads message updates available through the linked WhatsApp device and prepares daily export files on a Google Cloud virtual machine. These files can contain message text, dates, conversation identifiers, and other message metadata. Some message content may be unavailable from the linked device. The tool does not intentionally download media attachments.

Google Drive access

With the owner's authorization, the app requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses that permission to create a private folder and upload, inspect, or update its own export files in the owner's My Drive. The permission does not provide general access to unrelated Drive files. The app does not make the folder or exports public.

Storage and sharing

Message data and connection tokens are stored on the owner's virtual machine; exported files are stored in the owner's Google Drive after upload. Google Cloud, Google Drive, and WhatsApp provide the underlying services. This public website contains no message data. The app does not sell data or use it for advertising. It does not share exports with other users.

Security and retention

The tool restricts access to its local files and uses the owner's private Drive. These measures reduce access but do not guarantee absolute security. Files remain on the virtual machine or in Drive until the owner removes them or the underlying services remove them; there is no automatic deletion promise.

Owner controls

The owner can revoke the app's Google access through Google Account settings, unlink the WhatsApp device through WhatsApp, delete exports in Drive, and remove local files from the virtual machine. Revoking access stops future Drive uploads but does not automatically delete files already stored. For questions, use the support address shown on the Google authorization screen.
