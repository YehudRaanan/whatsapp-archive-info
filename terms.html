Terms of use

Last updated 12 October 2026

WhatsAppArchive Collector is a personal tool for its sole owner. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept public user accounts.

Purpose

The tool is intended to collect message updates from a WhatsApp device linked by the owner, prepare daily exports on the owner's virtual machine, and upload those exports to files it creates in the owner's private Google Drive when authorized.

Owner responsibility

The owner controls the linked WhatsApp device, Google authorization, cloud virtual machine, and stored exports. The owner is responsible for using the tool in accordance with applicable rules and the terms of the underlying Google and WhatsApp services.

Availability

Collection and export can be interrupted by service outages, device unlinking, expired access, storage limits, or changes to the underlying services. The tool is provided as is, without a promise of complete message coverage, continuous availability, or a particular retention period.

Stopping use

The owner may stop use by unlinking the WhatsApp device, revoking Google access, stopping the collector on the virtual machine, and deleting stored files. Revoking access does not automatically remove previously uploaded files.

This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or WhatsApp. See the privacy policy for data handling.
